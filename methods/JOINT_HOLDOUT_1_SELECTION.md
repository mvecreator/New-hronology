## Freeze rule

No member may be replaced after numerical scoring, even if:
- a control scores unexpectedly high;
- an NH-positive scores unexpectedly low;
- source complexity creates an inconvenient result.

Any defect discovered after scoring is recorded and deferred to a future gate.
